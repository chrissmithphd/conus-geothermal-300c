🌋 Interactive Map: Depth to 300°C Supercritical Geothermal Resources

Continental United States - Zoom, Pan, and Explore

About This Map

This interactive map shows the estimated drilling depth required to reach 300°C (supercritical geothermal conditions) across the continental United States. The analysis combines high-resolution Stanford thermal models (0-7 km) with digitized SMU temperature-at-depth maps (7.5-10 km).

How to Use:
  • Zoom: Scroll or use the zoom controls to zoom in/out
  • Pan: Click and drag to move around the map
  • Hover: Move your mouse over points to see detailed information
  • Toggle: Click legend items to show/hide depth categories

Key Finding: Only 17% of CONUS can access 300°C within 10 km drilling depth. The remaining 83% requires depths exceeding current technological and economic feasibility.
