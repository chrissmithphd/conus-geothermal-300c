### Key Insights

1. **No Shallow Resources**: Unlike conventional geothermal (150-200°C at 2-3 km), supercritical conditions (300°C) are NOT accessible at shallow depths anywhere in CONUS.

2. **Western US Concentration**: The accessible 17.2% (within 10 km) is almost entirely in:
   - Nevada (Basin & Range)
   - Eastern Oregon/Southern Idaho
   - Parts of Utah, New Mexico, California
   - Scattered locations in Wyoming (Yellowstone), Montana, Washington (Cascades)

3. **The "7 km Wall"**: Only 5% of CONUS reaches 300°C within 7 km (current proven drilling depth). The next 3 km (7-10 km) adds 12% more accessible area - a tripling of the resource base.

4. **Eastern US Challenge**: The entire eastern half of the country (everything east of approximately -100° longitude) requires >10 km drilling - beyond current practical limits.

5. **Technology Gap**: 83% of CONUS is currently inaccessible because it requires >10 km drilling, which has never been commercially demonstrated for geothermal projects.
